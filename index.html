Overview

Revenue forecast

Net income by month: actual received (past & month-to-date) plus projected renewals from active subscriptions, assuming no churn.

MRR over time

Net monthly recurring revenue at each month's end.

Net revenue per month

Actual money received, by charge month.

New vs. cancelled subscribers

Monthly subscription starts and cancellations.

Active subscribers by tier

Current paying subscriptions by plan.

Top countries

Customers and lifetime net revenue by country (top 10 by customer count).

New vs. returning revenue

Each month's net revenue split into first-ever charges (acquisition) and repeat charges (renewals & upgrades).

ARPU over time

Average net monthly revenue per active subscriber, at each month's end.

Cumulative net revenue

Running total of money received over time.

MRR by billing period

Net monthly recurring revenue from monthly vs. yearly plans.

MRR by plan

Net monthly recurring revenue contributed by each tier.

Renewals due soon

Active subscriptions scheduled to renew in the next 30 days.

Customer lifetime & LTV

All-time value per customer, and observed lifespan/value of subscribers who have already churned.

Discounts

Discount-code usage and how many subscribers with active access pay full price, a discounted price, or nothing.

Customers per discount code

Unique customers who have used each code (all time).

Subscriptions by price

Subscriptions with active access, by how much they pay.
